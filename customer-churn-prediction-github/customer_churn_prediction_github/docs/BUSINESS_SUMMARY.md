# Business Summary

## Problem
Customer churn can reduce recurring revenue and increase acquisition costs. The objective is to identify characteristics associated with churn and build a model that can prioritise customers for retention activity.

## Analytical approach
1. Audit data quality.
2. Explore churn across customer segments.
3. Engineer business-friendly features.
4. Compare Logistic Regression and Random Forest.
5. Evaluate precision, recall, F1 and ROC-AUC.
6. Create risk segments.
7. Translate findings into retention KPIs and actions.

## Business value
The model is a decision-support tool. A retention team could combine churn risk with customer value and campaign cost to prioritise interventions.

## Important distinction
Prediction identifies higher estimated risk; it does not establish causation. Retention actions should be tested for incremental impact.

## Suggested dashboard
Total customers, churn rate, high-risk customers, high-risk percentage, churn by contract, churn by tenure, churn by internet service, offer acceptance, campaign cost, value retained, model precision and recall.
